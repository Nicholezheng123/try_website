02 / OPTIMIZATION
UNIVERSITY EXAM SCHEDULING

Making Exam Scheduling Smarter

CORNELL UNIVERSITY · PROFESSOR DAVID SHMOYS

Exam scheduling affects students' workloads and well-being. I explored integer programming models that balance instructor preferences, exam conflicts and high-stress patterns while helping teams visualize alternative schedules.

PART A · PRELIMINARY EXAMS

Improving prelim schedules

We explored integer programming, parameter tuning, makeup options and exam-spacing visualizations. In the poster's specific experiments, removing a slot before spring break increased conflicts more than fourfold; a greedy splitting approach reduced conflicts to approximately half the original schedule.

These results describe particular tested schedules, not a general guarantee.

PART B · FINAL EXAMS

Intelligent block assignment

We compared three versus four daily exam slots using integer programming, weighted co-enrollment conflict penalties, and student workload metrics. The chart below shows the reported experimental comparison.

Three slots vs. four slots per day
